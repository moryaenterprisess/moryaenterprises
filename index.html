About us

Trusted for RO water solutions since 2015

Morya Enterprise was started in 2015 with a simple goal: to make clean drinking water easy to get for every household and business.

We stock a range of RO water purifiers for homes and commercial RO plants for shops, offices, schools and other places that need a larger supply of purified water. Tell us your water source and daily need, and we will help you pick a suitable system.

Water problems do not keep office hours, so neither do we. Call us any time, day or night.
